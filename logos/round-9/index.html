Coma Toast.

Comatose, but toast: one plain slice that’s out cold, with closed-line eyes and the same tiny straight mouth every time. Each option gives it a different sleepy, knocked-out or hospital accessory, from a nightcap and a sleep mask to an IV drip and a heart-monitor line.
