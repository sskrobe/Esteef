Brand identity · Round 11 · Coma Toast · 15 toppers

Heaven, hell, or toast.

The face is settled: X’d-out eyes and a heartbeat for a mouth. This is every combination of four toppers: halo, devil horns, angel wings and devil wings. #1 to #4 are each piece on its own, #5 and #6 are the full angel and full devil, and #7 to #15 mix them up. Toppers sit tight to the toast and the wings tuck close, so every outline stays compact.
