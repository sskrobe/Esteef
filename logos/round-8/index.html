Brand identity · Round 8 · 3 kept + 12 new

Straight face. Good hat.

Built on the three you kept (Daily Bread, Off Duty and The Inspector): a plain shape and one strong accessory. Every face now has the same tiny straight-line mouth, so they look completely unbothered. More guinea pigs, and much more food: pretzel, croissant, onigiri, fried egg, toast, pizza and dumpling. Daily Bread and Twisted stay faceless on purpose, so the crown does all the talking.
