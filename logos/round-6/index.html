Brand identity · Round 6 · 16 kept + 10 new

Too cute to care.

Every Round 5 mark is kept, and the set is now weapon-free: Ronin swaps its katana for a calligraphy brush. Ten new marks push the attitude further: expensive, snobby, and in on the joke, with names to match (Cold Shoulder, Spill the Tea, Boo-jee, Old Money). Every mark still splits into a head plus one signature second shape. Check them with Outline test.
