Brand identity · Round 4 · 8 refined + 8 new

Too cute to care.

The mood: high fashion with an attitude. Expensive, a little snobby, and in on the joke. Every character now gives you the unimpressed side-eye instead of a scowl. The shapes follow the Psycho Bunny formula: a head you can’t mistake, over two crossed objects that set the mood. Turn on Outline test to strip out the inside details. If a mark still reads as just an outline, it works as a logo.
