Brand identity · Round 2 · 4 kept + 10 new

Cute face, bad intentions.

Your four keepers from Round 1, plus ten new marks aimed at guys 20 to 40 into MMA, gaming and hip hop. The cast now includes characters that aren’t animals (an alien, a ghost, a reaper, a slice of toast, a money bag). Several play on Daily Bread, with bread as money. The new marks use heavier shapes and wider cut-outs so they read clearly from a distance, like Nine Lives.
