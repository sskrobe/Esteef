Brand identity · Round 1 · 10 concepts

Cute animal, bad intentions.

Ten mascot marks in the spirit of Psycho Bunny: a sweet animal with a villain twist, kept lighthearted. Emblems are simple enough to embroider on a chest. Graphics carry more detail for screen-printed fronts and backs. All are one color for now so you can judge shape before palette.

Shirt color
Shortlist up to 3 favorites
01

Captain Quack

Duckling×PirateEmblem

The closest cousin to Psycho Bunny: one face over crossed bones. A duckling with an eyepatch and a scowl reads at 1 inch on a chest.

02

Slow Burn

Sloth×DevilishGraphic

A sloth’s natural eye stripes already look sly. Horns, a fang and a pointed tail push it into troublemaker without losing the sleepy charm.

03

Otter Job

Sea otter×HeistEmblem

A sea otter in a ski mask with shifty eyes and whiskers poking out. It nods to the beachwear crowd you liked in the whale brand, with a bank-job twist.

04

The Don

Koala×MobsterGraphic

A koala in a fedora with heavy-lidded eyes and a toothpick: a mob boss who’d still nap 20 hours a day. The big ears and nose make a strong silhouette.

05

Count Nibbles

Hamster×VampireEmblem

A round hamster with full cheeks, a widow’s peak, fangs and a Dracula collar. Cute first, menacing second.

06

Bamboo Bandit

Panda×OutlawGraphic

The panda’s eye patches become aviator shades, with a stitched scar and a smirk. A strong pick for big back prints.

07

Nine Lives

Kitten×PirateEmblem

A kitten skull over crossed fish skeletons. It’s the easiest to recognize as a skull-and-crossbones mark, and the big-eye glints keep it cute.

08

Black Sheep

Lamb×DevilishGraphic

A fluffy lamb with devil horns and a pitchfork. "Black sheep" is a phrase everyone knows and suits a brand with an attitude.

09

Most Wanted

Puppy×BanditEmblem

A floppy-eared pup in a cowboy hat and polka-dot bandana with an angry unibrow. A wanted-poster puppy, and it fits well in a small chest logo.

10

Happy Accident

Quokka×ChaosGraphic

The quokka is called the world’s happiest animal. Here it grins while holding a lit cartoon bomb, which suits a graphic tee.
