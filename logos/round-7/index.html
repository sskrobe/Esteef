Brand identity · Round 7 · 10 new, less cute

Plain animal. Loud accessory.

Taking the cute out. Like the Psycho Bunny rabbit, each head is now a plain, generic silhouette: dot eyes, no expressions. The accessory carries the identity: a crown, a detective’s hat, a top hat, a beret. Rounds 1 to 6 are still saved. Use Outline test to check the shapes.
