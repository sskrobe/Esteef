Baguette ("Bag-Ette") · Round 1

Female mascot concepts: French, sassy, a baguette mixed with a character. Each shown on cream and on black.

1. Renarde

The baguette is the fox: a loaf standing up with a slight lean, fox ears and a red beret on top, a bushy fox tail curling out behind, and the side-eye smirk.

2. Fox Loaf

One loaf, one fox, in profile: the pointed end is her snout (black nose tip), ears and a navy beret at the head end, the scores run down her back like fur, and the far end turns into a white-tipped tail.

3. Bag-Ette

The pun, literally: a baguette that is a designer handbag. Gold strap with a red bow, gold clasp, and the face right on the bag. Chic, a little bougie.

4. Fox à la Baguette

A fox head front-on, red beret cocked to one side, with a baguette slung behind her like a sword. The clearest "fox" read of the set.

5. Minette

A cat-baguette lounging on a diagonal: cat ears, a tiny navy beret, cat-eye eyeliner, whiskers, and a red ribbon tied around the loaf.

6. Mademoiselle

The full diva, no animal: a standing baguette in oversized cat-eye sunglasses, one brow up over the frames, red beret, red lips, and a Breton-stripe scarf knotted at the neck.

7. Bijou

A poodle-baguette: a fluffy pink poodle head and pompom tail on a baguette body, pearl collar, tiny navy beret, maximum attitude.
