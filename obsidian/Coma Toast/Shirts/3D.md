# 3D

Part of [[Coma Toast]]. The old-school **red/blue 3D-movie (anaglyph)** look, after a Psycho Bunny tee with a stacked brand-name block behind the character.

- **Status:** v1 made 2026-10-13.
- **Local folder:** `G:\ComaToast\3D\`
- **Repo folder:** `tshirts/3d/`

## Look
- **Behind:** **DAILY / BREAD / DAILY / BREAD** stacked as one justified block (Montserrat Black, each line stretched to the same width), printed twice: a **red** `#ff2a3d` copy shifted left and a **cyan** `#00b4ff` copy shifted right (about 0.23 in each way), as if seen without the glasses. Where they overlap, a third color.
- **In front:** the **Saint** mark (main halo logo), solid, with its own smaller red/cyan fringe, and a clear gap cut around it so it pops off the text. It covers the middle of the text block, like the reference character.
- Solid colors only (no transparency), DTG-safe.

| Version | Overlap | Mark | Face | Shirts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3D Light** (`light`) | near-black `#1d1330` (like ink) | black | white | light |
| **3D Dark** (`dark`) | white (like light) | white | black | dark |

## Size and placement (chest standard)
Bella+Canvas 3001 (Printful product 71), DTG front: **10.5 × 11.05 in**, centered, top **1.5 in below the collar seam**.

## Back: $Daily Bread$
`$DAILY BREAD$` in Montserrat Black with the same red/cyan offset and the mark's core color on top, about 7.05 × 0.76 in, in the bottom-right corner of the 12 × 16 in back print area.

## Files (`files/<light|dark>/`)
- `front.png` (trimmed, 300 dpi, transparent), `back.png` (whole 12 × 16 in back area; fill it).
- `3d-samples.png`: each version front and back on its shirt, plus close-ups.

## Printful setup
1. **Front print:** upload `front.png`, set it 10.5 in wide, center it, top 1.5 in below the collar seam.
2. **Back print:** upload `back.png` and let it fill the area.

## Regenerating
`node tshirts/3d/3d.js` (needs Node and Playwright, or `playwright-core` with `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` and `BROWSER_CHANNEL=msedge`). Colors, offsets and the mark size are in the script.
