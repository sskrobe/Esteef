All share the toast, X'd-out eyes and the **Soft Pulse** mouth (Round 13, option E). Files: `logos/final/` (SVG, 3000 px PNG, and `coma-toast-logos.zip` with 6000 px transparent PNGs at 300 dpi), black and white.

## How we got here
1. **Rounds 1–3:** Psycho Bunny–style cute animals with a villain twist, then MMA / gaming / hip hop marks, then food characters. Favorites at the time: Nine Lives, Bamboo Bandit, The Don, Captain Quack, Ground & Pound, Toast.
2. **Rounds 4–6:** one formula (distinctive head + second section), each mark given its own second section; weapons removed.
3. **Rounds 7–8:** strip the cute: plain heads where the accessory carries the mark (crown, detective hat). Daily Bread, Off Duty and The Inspector kept; straight-line mouths added.
4. **Round 9:** "Coma Toast": ten toast options with sleep and hospital accessories.
5. **Round 10:** X'd-out eyes + heartbeat line.
6. **Rounds 11–12:** every combination of halo, horns, angel wings, devil wings (15 marks); a more mouth-like heartbeat.
7. **Round 13:** mouth study; **Soft Pulse** chosen. Saint, Little Devil, Heaven Sent and Raising Hell chosen as the go-forward designs, plus the plain toast.
