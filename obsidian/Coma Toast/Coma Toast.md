# Coma Toast

Clothing brand project: t-shirts and sweatshirts. This is the hub note; each shirt design has its own note under [[#Shirts]].

## Brief
- **Audience:** leans male, 20–40.
- **Culture:** MMA, gaming, hip hop, urban.
- **Mood:** high fashion, "I'm better than you", snarky, tongue in cheek, expensive.
- **Products:** t-shirts and sweatshirts. Printing through Printful (all-over print tees so far).
- **Names in play:** "Coma Toast" (the mascot and mark) and "Daily Bread" (bread = money; used as the `$Daily Bread$` wordmark on [[PaintShop]]).
- **Rules for the marks:** no weapons; compact, balanced, symmetrical; a distinctive outline that reads as a silhouette; straight, expressionless mouth.

## The mark
A plain slice of toast, knocked out: X'd-out eyes and a "Soft Pulse" heartbeat line for a mouth. See [[Logo Marks]] for the official versions and how we got there.

## Shared design choices
- **Every design gets a ` Bread$` back print** (standing rule): bottom right of the back, as low as the product's back print area allows, in that design's art style.
- **Fonts:** drip/graffiti fonts (Rubik Wet Paint) are design-specific. Labels and other brand pieces use the corporate **brand font: Montserrat ExtraBold**, in capitals (see [[Labels]]).
- Shirt base color for dark tees: `#121214` (near-black).
- Colorways used so far: Hot Wire (`#ff2e88` pink + `#c6ff00` lime), Toxic (`#ffe600` yellow + `#00e5ff` cyan), Riot (`#00e5ff` cyan + `#ff2e88` pink). White accent `#f4f4f2`.
- Printful All-Over Print Men's Crew Neck (product 257) print files: front and back 28 × 36 in (4200 × 5400 px), sleeves 20 × 12 in (3000 × 1800 px), 150 dpi. The collar band is cut from the top 2.3 in of the front file, so keep that strip plain. Full template measurements are in [[PaintShop]]. Always check placement in Printful's mockup preview before ordering.
