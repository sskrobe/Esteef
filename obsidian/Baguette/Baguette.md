# Baguette

A new mascot series after [[Coma Toast]]. The play on words is **"Bag-Ette"**, so the character is **female**: a French theme with a lot of sass and attitude, mixing a baguette with an animal (a fox was the first idea) or something else.

- **Local folder:** `G:\ComaToast\Baguette\`
- **Repo folder:** `baguette/logos/round-1/` (`concepts.js` has the artwork, `build.js` builds `index.html`, `svg/` and `baguette-round-1.png`)

## Shared look
Same drawing language as the Coma Toast marks (bold ink outlines, flat fills, a simple silhouette), so the two brands feel related. Every concept shares one **sassy face kit**: half-lidded side-eye with lashes (or a cat-eye wing), one raised brow, a red-lipstick smirk, a beauty mark, pink blush. French cues: beret (red or navy), Breton stripes, red / navy / cream palette with a golden crust.

## Round 1 (2026-10-14): seven concepts
1. **Renarde:** the baguette *is* the fox: a standing loaf with a lean, fox ears, a red beret, a bushy fox tail curling out behind.
2. **Fox Loaf:** one loaf, one fox, in profile: the pointed end is her snout (black nose tip), ears and a navy beret at the head end, scores down her back like fur, the far end turns into a white-tipped tail.
3. **Bag-Ette:** the pun, literally: a baguette that is a designer handbag (gold strap, red bow, gold clasp), face on the bag.
4. **Fox à la Baguette:** a fox head front-on, red beret cocked, a baguette slung behind her like a sword. The clearest "fox" read.
5. **Minette:** a cat-baguette lounging on a diagonal: cat ears, tiny navy beret, cat-eye eyeliner, whiskers, red ribbon around the loaf.
6. **Mademoiselle:** the full diva, no animal: oversized cat-eye sunglasses, one brow up, red beret and lips, Breton-stripe scarf.
7. **Bijou:** a poodle-baguette: fluffy pink poodle head and pompom tail on a baguette body, pearl collar, tiny navy beret.

Faces were enlarged after the first render so the attitude reads at logo size.

## Notes
- **Trademark heads-up:** Fendi sells a famous handbag called the "Baguette". Worth a trademark search before using "Baguette" / "Bag-Ette" as an apparel brand name.
- Back-print rule from Coma Toast (`$Daily Bread$`) may or may not carry over to Baguette; to confirm when we get to shirts.
