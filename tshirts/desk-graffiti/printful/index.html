The desk tee, now with a mascot.

The school-desk all-over print with Raising Hell on the left chest. Everything else on the shirt is pen, pencil and carving. The logo is drawn in thick black marker over its own dab of white-out, so it’s the one deliberate mark on a desk full of scribbles and stands out while still looking hand-drawn.

Setting it up

  1. In Printful, open the All-Over Print Men’s Crew Neck T-Shirt (or the all-over tee you’re using) in the design maker.
  2. Upload front.jpg to the front, back.jpg to the back, and the sleeve files to each sleeve. Set each to fill its area.
  3. On the front, add raising-hell-patch.png as a second layer. Place it on the wearer’s left chest (your right as you look at the shirt), about 3.5 to 4 in wide.
  4. Check the mockup preview, especially that the patch isn’t too close to the collar or the armhole seam.
