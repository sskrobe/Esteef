Coma Toast · color-splash tee · review before final files

Three colorways, one back to sign off.

Three versions of the tee, one per colorway. Each one paints its blocks and spatter in its own two colors plus white. The front logo is the plain toast with no halo, slightly bigger and longer, with its top edge where it was. On the back, the big middle block is now two smaller staggered blocks, and $Daily Bread$ sits at the bottom right. Pick a font below to see it on every back.

Back font

What changed on the back

  • The one big center block is now two mid-sized blocks, staggered across the upper middle.
  • $Daily Bread$ sits at the bottom right, about 5.6 in wide, roughly 3.8 in in from the right edge and 3.2 in up from the bottom of the print file. The words are in the toast color and the dollar signs in the accent color.
  • Paint keeps a dark margin around the wordmark so it reads cleanly.

The shirt mockups assume the print file maps edge to edge onto the shirt piece. Printful's template isn't reachable from here, so turn on "Flat back panels" to see the full file, and I'll confirm placement against Printful's preview in the final step.
