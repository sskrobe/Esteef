Coma Toast · color-splash tee · review before final files

The toast, front and center.

Three versions of the tee, one per colorway, each painted in its own two colors plus white. The front logo is now 10 in tall (about 9.7 in wide), with its top edge where it was. It sits in a clean dark window, with the paint ringing it like a frame, so the toast is the centerpiece. The back has two staggered mid-sized blocks and $Daily Bread$ at the bottom right.
