Coma Toast · Printful all-over print

Saint, wired.

A near-black all-over print tee. Saint sits large in the middle of the chest, inverted: the shirt fills the mark and the color is only the line work, like a neon sign. Chunky square paint blocks, chips and drips cover the front, back and sleeves, with a dark margin left around the logo so it stays the hero.

Logo colorways

The front file uses Hot Wire. Each colorway also comes as a transparent outline PNG, so you can swap it in on Printful.

Setting it up

  1. Open the All-Over Print Men’s Crew Neck T-Shirt in Printful’s design maker.
  2. Upload front.jpg, back.jpg and the two sleeve files to their placements, each set to fill its area.
  3. Check that the logo sits centered on the chest with clear space under the collar. If it’s off, use front-no-logo.jpg and add a logo PNG as a layer, about 8 in wide, centered on the chest.
  4. Set the collar, if your product has a separate collar placement, to the same near-black (#121214).
